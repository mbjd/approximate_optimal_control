"TO. widespread practice, explicitly parameterising the trajectory solving
the resulting optimisaiton problem, usually w gradient based methods.
nonconvex so locally optimal solution. on the upside, depending on specific
discretisation, solution can be very close to continuous time solution."

"DP. in discrete time, well known, bellman iteration. in continuous
time/infinite horizon, bellman -> hjb. find some function that solves hjb
eq. practical main choice: how to parameterise function? common choices
are: linear comb of polynomials, or NN. both of these are investigated in a
relatively substantial body of literature. but practical applications are
still limited."

"policy optimisation: different paradigm, like approx DP at the heart of
many RL algorithms. but continuous time sparse literature."
